Account types

Computer administrators can install programs, change system-wide settings and manage other accounts. Limited accounts can use programs and change their own settings, picture and password.

Passwords

An account without a password logs on with a single click on the Welcome screen. If only one account exists and it has no password, Whistler logs on automatically, just like Windows XP.

Changing your picture

Open Control Panel → User Accounts, click your account, then Change my picture.
